THE VISION
The Player Portfolio is a player's public highlight page. Anyone with the link, signed in or not, opens it and watches the player's best moments. When a clip is shared to the portfolio, it must play there exactly as the player cut it in My Clips: the same few seconds, the same crop and pan, the same framing. It plays instantly and cleanly, like a highlight reel, with no black screens, dead buttons or confusing states. That is the bar: a stranger taps a clip and it simply plays.
