screen too long, its close (X) button has poor contrast against the 
green background, and its padding/placement feels awkward, sometimes 
covering the clip preview underneath.

1. State the current auto-dismiss duration, then reduce it to roughly

2. Increase the close button's contrast (darker icon and/or a subtle

   preview beneath it.

save/publish behavior.
