── THE MODEL (as demonstrated in the mock) ──
Subscriptions are expiry-date based. Each player has a subscription expiry
date. Past it, debt accrues automatically: one effective monthly fee per full
month late, COMPUTED from the expiry date and today — never materialized by
background jobs. Effective fee = team's monthly fee − player's fixed JOD
discount (never below zero). A player's outstanding balance = accrued months
× effective fee + their unpaid one-off fees.

── TAB 1: PLAYER PAYMENTS ──
- Teams view: one card per team (= existing academy_squads) showing player
  count, monthly fee, players behind, outstanding total. "+ Add team" creates
  a real squad (name + monthly fee) — the same squads the Squads section
  shows.
- Team page (drill-down with back): every active player of the team. Each row:
  name; effective fee with discount shown if any; status pill — green "Paid
  until <date>", amber "Expires <date>" within 7 days, red "Expired <date> ·
  N mo owed"; outstanding amount (or "Settled"); three actions: Renew,
  Other fees, ✎ billing.
  Inactive players appear ONLY if they still have an outstanding balance,
  clearly badged inactive.
- "+ Add player" on the team page creates a REAL roster player (the same
  academy_players the Players section shows) with name, optional discount,
  and a "paid until" date picker defaulting to one month from today, freely
  editable. Existing roster players with no expiry set yet appear with a
  "No subscription yet" state and get their date via ✎.
- Renew dialog: months stepper 1–12, defaulting to the months owed (min 1);
  warning line when months are owed ("Owes N months since <date> — covered
  first"); live calculation (months × effective fee) and the new expiry date;
  renewal always extends FROM THE CURRENT EXPIRY, so paying late never erases
  the gap. Recording it creates a payment record (for history and the
  dashboard) and advances the expiry.
- Other fees dialog: the player's one-off charges — reuse the EXISTING
  academy_fees table and records for this — list with Paid pill or "Mark
  paid" per fee, and an add form (label + amount). Marking paid counts as
  collected money.
- ✎ Billing dialog: fixed JOD/month discount, and a directly editable
  subscription expiry date (for corrections — injured month, special deal);
  owed months recalculate from it.

── TAB 2: SALARIES ──
Employee list (a new academy staff concept): name, role, salary JOD/month,
next salary date (red when due/past), contract end date (amber warning
within 30 days). Actions per row: "Pay salary" — records the payment as a
PAID entry under Other payments with a Salary category and advances the next
salary date one month — and "Edit" (all fields). "+ Add employee" with the
same dialog. Nothing is ever paid automatically.

── TAB 3: OTHER PAYMENTS ──
- Quick-add form: category chips (defaults: Rent, Transport, Replay,
  Equipment, Other, Salary) with a dashed "+ type" chip that lets the owner
  save a custom category (per academy, persisted); label; amount; status
  Paid / Not paid.
- Month list: each payment with category, date, amount, Paid/Not-paid pill,
  "Mark paid" on unpaid rows, and a repeat action that re-records the same
  payment today as NOT PAID. Header shows paid and unpaid totals for the
  month.
