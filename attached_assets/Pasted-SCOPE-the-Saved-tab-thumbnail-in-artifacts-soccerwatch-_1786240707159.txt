--- THE BUG (already diagnosed, do not re-investigate) ---

LocalClipCard derives its thumbnail with:
    record.playbackUrl.replace("/playlist.m3u8", "/thumbnail.jpg")

Created clips store a PROXIED playback URL shaped like
    /api/hls-proxy/manifest?url=https%3A%2F%2F...%2Fplaylist.m3u8
The playlist path is URL-encoded as %2Fplaylist.m3u8, so the literal
replacement never matches. The <img> src therefore points at an HLS manifest,
which cannot render as an image. Legacy raw URLs fail differently — they hit
Bunny directly and get a 403 because of Bunny's Referer restriction.
