"Fees & Payments" is the owner's money view for the academy. One sidebar item,
three clearly separated categories inside it:

1. PLAYER PAYMENTS (money in) — fees charged to players, organized BY SQUAD:
   each squad is its own group (with the squad name and a per-squad subtotal
   of due/paid/waived), players' fees listed inside their squad's group, and

   actions per fee: mark paid, waive, edit, delete, add fee.
2. SALARIES (money out) — payments to staff/coaches: payee name, amount,
   date, due/paid status, optional note. Add, edit, mark paid, delete.
3. OTHER PAYMENTS (money out) — any other academy expense: label, amount,
