── CHANGE 1: THE CURRENT MONTH IS LIVE; ONLY PAST MONTHS ARE FROZEN ──
This is the most important change and it is a semantics fix, not a UI fix.
Today the current month is a frozen snapshot, which produces absurd results:
I set a squad's rate and this month doesn't react; I add a staff member and
this month shows "no salary payments"; only 2 of 16 players appear because
the month materialized before rates existed; a player I marked exempt still
shows a stale Due row.

New rule:
- The CURRENT month (and any future month, if opened) continuously reflects
  today's billing rules: setting or changing a squad rate, adding a player,
  changing an override/exemption, adding or editing staff — all of it shows
  up in the current month immediately on next load (refresh the month's rows
  idempotently when it is viewed or when rules change).
- Rows already marked PAID or WAIVED in the current month are never altered
  by a rules refresh — payments that happened stay exactly as recorded.
- PAST months stay frozen exactly as the snapshot model already does. The
  effective-dated terms and history hooks stay — they are what makes past
  months correct.
- With this rule, the "This month is a fixed snapshot..." banner becomes
  unnecessary — remove it. If any note is needed, a single quiet line on
  PAST months only ("Closed month") is enough.
