---
name: Replay 4K fallback boundaries
description: Product and privacy boundaries for original 4K playback.
---

Original 4K playback is an optional alternative for recorded-match playback, admin recorded previews, and owner-share watch pages. Keep live video, exports, downloads, reels, and Find Yourself playback on Bunny Stream. Owner-share 4K playlists and media must remain behind the active share token; never expose the raw Storage media URL to the browser.

**Why:** The requested feature is limited to recorded-match viewing and should not alter established live, export, or self-identification workflows. Owner-shared footage can remain private even though it is publicly accessible through a share link.

**How to apply:** When adding a new player or media path, confirm it is one of the approved recorded-playback surfaces. Preserve Stream for other paths and use token-gated proxy routes for owner-share media.
